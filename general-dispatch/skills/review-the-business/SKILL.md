# Review the business

Read the schedule (`get_schedule` for today and tomorrow), what is waiting on a person (`list_tasks`: approvals, escalations, call-backs), open estimates (`list_jobs`, by `quote_status`), and recent activity (`list_events`). The MCP resource `skill://morning-brief` shows what the daily brief covers and in what order. Lead with what needs the owner, in plain words; approvals and takeovers are the owner's to decide on their owner page, not yours.
